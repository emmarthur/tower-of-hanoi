How to play

Goal

Move all five disks from tower A to B or C, keeping the largest disk on the bottom and never placing a larger disk on a smaller one.

Moves

Tap a tower with disks to pick it up, then tap the tower where the top disk should go. Only the top disk of a tower can move. An empty tower accepts any disk.

Score

The fewest moves for five disks is 31 (2ⁿ − 1). Your move count is shown above the board.
